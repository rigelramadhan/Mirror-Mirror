Mirror Mirror hardened - portable Windows x64

Extract the ZIP and run MirrorMirrorHardened.exe. No Python installation needed.
The unsigned executable runs as your normal Windows user.

PC: open http://localhost:5000 in Chrome or Edge.
iPhone: use the HTTPS address displayed in the PC page or tray menu (port 5443).
Your phone and PC must be on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Restrict the Windows firewall
exception to Private networks and your viewing device; never port-forward 5443.
Port 5000 listens only on the PC itself.

ONE-TIME IPHONE SETUP:
The PC page has an iPhone HTTPS setup section. Download the public certificate
profile there and transfer it directly to your iPhone using a trusted method,
such as your own iCloud Drive or a cable. Open it on the phone, install it under
Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, then enable full trust for the
matching Mirror Mirror Local certificate under General > About > Certificate
Trust Settings. Check the certificate name/fingerprint against the PC page.
This adds a local certificate authority to your phone; remove its profile when
you stop using this app. Never bypass Safari certificate warnings. See README.md.
No certificate is automatically installed on either device.

SHARING:
1. On the PC page, click Share Screen and select the screen/window.
2. On the phone, open the displayed HTTPS address in Safari and enter the code.
3. Back on the PC, choose Allow view only, Allow view + mouse, or Deny.
4. Disconnects revoke approval. To reconnect, start sharing again with a new code.
5. Sessions expire after 15 minutes without mouse activity or 60 minutes total.
6. Use Disconnect or tray menu > Stop sharing now / Quit when finished.

SAFARI MOUSE CONTROLS (only after PC approval of mouse control):
One-finger drag moves the pointer; tap left-clicks; two-finger tap right-clicks;
two-finger drag scrolls. Keyboard input and click-and-drag are not implemented.
Moving the real PC pointer into a corner activates the mouse fail-safe and ends
the session when the next remote mouse command arrives. The tray stop action
revokes access immediately.

Private TLS keys are generated separately on your PC, protected for your Windows
account and SYSTEM, and are never included in this ZIP or served to the phone.
Do not share files from %LOCALAPPDATA%\MirrorMirror\tls. Only the public profile
downloaded from the PC page is intended for transfer. A changed PC IP address
requires restarting the app; the root certificate normally stays the same.

Security tests and packaged startup checks do not constitute a full security
audit or a guarantee against compromise. Live iPhone screen sharing has not been
verified on your device. Source: https://github.com/rigelramadhan/Mirror-Mirror
